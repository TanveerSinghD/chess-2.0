good answers.

Can I play with a friend?+

Yes. Player versus player lets you take turns on the same device. Pass the board between you and enjoy a game together.

What if I’m playing on my own?+

Choose Play Bot for a solo game. You play White, and the bot plays Black.

Can I revisit earlier moves?+

Use the back and forward controls to review earlier positions. Return to the latest position to keep playing. The live clock keeps running while you review.

Is the app available to download?+

Chess 2.0 is currently in development. An App Store link will appear here when the app is available.
