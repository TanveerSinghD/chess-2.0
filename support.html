CHESS 2.0 · IPHONE & IPAD

Support

A little help for your next game. Find answers or get in touch with the developer.

Contact the developer

Need help or found a bug? Send a support request to the Chess 2.0 developer through the project’s GitHub issue tracker.

Send a support request ↗

A free GitHub account is required. Requests are public, so do not include passwords, Apple Account details, private contact information, or personal photos. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, game mode, and the steps that caused the issue.

Play together on one device

Tap Play, choose Pass and Play, select a variant and time control, then tap Start. Take turns on the same iPhone or iPad.

Play with a friend online

Choose Play Friend on the New Game screen. Connect to Game Center if prompted, allow friends access if you want to use the friend list, and choose a friend. Select your variant and time control, then tap Start to send a challenge.

If no friends appear, use Add Friends and check that Game Center is signed in in your device’s Settings. Both players need an internet connection and compatible app versions. For Apple Account or Game Center account issues, visit Apple Support.

Choose a different kind of chess

  • Royal Rumble: familiar pieces with changed movement and special abilities.
  • Artillery: play with the Nuke, Knook, Night Rider, and Protector.
  • Secret Queen: choose a pawn to hide a second Queen before play begins.
  • Extended: classic armies on an 8 × 12 board.
  • Draft: build an army from a lone King; choose an 8 × 8 or 8 × 12 board.

Use the information button beside the selected variant for its rules. For solo play, choose Play Bot.

Review moves and clocks

Use the left and right arrows during a game to revisit earlier positions. Return to the latest position before making another move. Reviewing moves does not pause a live game’s clock.

Reset your local profile

Open Settings and choose Delete Account to reset your local profile and app preferences. This clears local app data and returns to onboarding; it does not delete your Apple Account or Game Center identity. Save anything you want to keep before confirming the reset.

Something is not working?

Close and reopen the app, check for app updates, and include reproducible steps in your support request. For online play, also check the internet connection and Game Center sign-in. Avoid reinstalling before preserving any local data you need.

Read the Terms of Service. The app’s Privacy Policy is available in Settings.
