CHESS 2.0 · IPHONE & IPAD

Privacy Policy

Your local profile stays on your device. Online friend matches use Apple Game Center.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes Chess 2.0 for iPhone and iPad, developed by Tanveer Singh Dhaliwal, and this supporting website.

Information on your device

The app stores your chosen local display name, avatar or selected photo, onboarding status, board and piece themes, sound and haptic preferences, and recent Game Center opponent identifiers on your device. Board positions, move history and clocks are used to run your games. Your local profile and custom avatar are not uploaded to a developer-operated server.

A photo is used only if you choose one for your avatar. The app does not upload your photo library or access your device’s address book.

Game Center and online matches

Local pass-and-play and bot games do not require Game Center. If you choose online play, Apple handles Game Center sign-in. The app uses the public Game Center alias, player identifier and available player image supplied by GameKit to display players and arrange matches.

With your permission, the app accesses Game Center friends to show available friends for challenges. During a match, game setup, piece moves, draft choices, special actions, clocks and match-control messages are exchanged with the other player through GameKit peer connections or Apple’s relay services. This data is used to run the match. The developer does not maintain an external database of player identities or gameplay.

Apple’s services and their own data handling are governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy and Game Center settings.

Advertising and tracking

Chess 2.0 does not include advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs, use the advertising identifier, track you across other companies’ apps or websites, sell personal data, or request your location.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit your local profile, replace or remove your custom avatar, and change preferences in the app. Settings → Delete Account resets the app’s local profile and preferences and returns to onboarding. It does not delete your Apple Account or Game Center identity. Manage those services through Apple. Device backups are managed separately by your device and Apple backup settings.

You can play locally without giving access to Game Center friends. Manage Game Center and photo permissions in your device settings.

This website and support requests

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include developer-added analytics, advertising, or data-collection forms. GitHub may process technical information when serving the site under its Privacy Statement.

Support requests are made through GitHub’s issue tracker and are public. Information you choose to post there is processed by GitHub and can be read by the developer and others. Please avoid posting private contact details, passwords, personal photos or Apple Account information. Include only the device and app details needed to explain your issue.

Children and families

The app does not ask for a date of birth or add advertising or tracking. Parents and guardians can manage Apple services and Game Center access using Apple’s family and device controls.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app develops; the date above identifies this version. For privacy questions or help with local data, visit the support page. Read the Terms of Service for app usage terms.
